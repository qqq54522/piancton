# 图片二级卖点打标 Skill

用途：依据图片证据，在封闭的 16 个二级标签中判断主标签和副标签。

## 图片类型与证据

## Image types and evidence

- Function image: clear interface, button, module, or data. Prefer A evidence.
- Emotion-led scene: outcome and human feeling are central. Use B+C evidence.
- Function-led scene: people plus a partial interface. Use A+B evidence.
- A: direct feature evidence such as a report page, camera frame, teacher
  message, error list, test result, textbook chapter, plan, animation, or
  similar-question recommendation.
- B: behavior evidence such as photographing a paper, viewing a report,
  talking with a teacher, previewing before class, or self-study.
- C: outcome evidence such as relief, confidence, initiative, transparency,
  reduced anxiety, or exam calmness.

## Closed labels

- 同步规划体系 > AI定制学习方案: personalized goals, path, schedule, daily tasks.
- 同步伴学体系 > 学情报告反馈: duration, accuracy, progress, mastery, weakness, advice.
- 同步伴学体系 > 真人老师督学: human teacher reminders, follow-up, feedback, supervision.
- 同步培养体系 > 学段衔接: 小升初、初升高、new-stage transition.
- 同步培养体系 > 万能解法: general method, underlying logic, reusable model.
- 同步培养体系 > 专家规划: expert, teaching-research, examiner, textbook-author backing.
- 同步自学体系 > AI错题本: error collection, cause analysis, review, similar questions.
- 同步自学体系 > AI拍题精学: photograph a problem and receive guided explanation.
- 同步自学体系 > 极速预习复习: rapid pre-class preview or post-class review.
- 同步自学体系 > AI私教答疑: AI dialogue and on-demand tutoring, not a human teacher.
- 同步考点体系 > 专项培优: focused difficult-point improvement.
- 同步考点体系 > 新课标新考法预测: new curriculum, new context, new question forms.
- 同步考点体系 > 举一反三: transfer through similar and variant questions.
- 同步校内体系 > 课后小测: test immediately after learning and result checking.
- 同步校内体系 > 同步校内: textbook edition, school chapter, daily classroom alignment.
- 同步校内体系 > 动画精讲: animation or story-based explanation of abstract knowledge.

## Redundancy boundary

Allow one primary and one or two secondary labels only when separate evidence
supports them. A child studying does not imply every self-study label; a happy
parent does not imply every companion label; a phone does not imply photo
learning; a paper does not imply every exam label.
